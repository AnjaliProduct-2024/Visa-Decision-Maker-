# Product Case Study: AI-Powered Fraud Detection with Visa Decision Manager

## Business Challenge

A global airline, **SkyConnect Airlines**, saw a sharp rise in payment fraud after shifting 70% of its ticket sales online. Its existing rule-based fraud system struggled to keep up, resulting in:

* Chargeback rates increasing to **1.45%**
* **12% of transactions** requiring manual review
* High false declines affecting genuine customers
* Revenue loss from fraudulent and blocked transactions

---

## Solution: Visa Decision Manager

Visa implemented **Visa Decision Manager (VDM)**, an AI-powered fraud prevention platform that evaluates transaction risk in real time.

### Key Features

* Real-time AI risk scoring for every transaction
* Custom fraud rules and velocity checks
* Device fingerprinting to detect suspicious activity
* 3-D Secure authentication for medium-risk transactions
* Case management and fraud reporting tools

The solution was integrated through APIs and optimized over a six-week period.

---

## How It Works

1. **Capture:** Collect transaction, device, and behavioural data.
2. **Enrich:** Compare against Visa's global fraud intelligence.
3. **Score:** AI assigns a fraud risk score within milliseconds.
4. **Decide:** Automatically approve, decline, or request additional authentication.
5. **Learn:** Models continuously improve using fraud outcomes and chargeback data.

---

## Results Achieved (Within 90 Days)

| Metric          | Before | After |
| --------------- | -----: | ----: |
| Chargeback Rate |  1.45% | 0.87% |
| Manual Reviews  |    12% |  3.2% |
| False Positives |   8.4% |  4.0% |
| Decline Rate    |   9.3% |  6.2% |

### Business Impact

* **40% reduction** in chargebacks
* **73% reduction** in manual reviews
* **52% fewer false positives**
* **3.1% increase** in approval rates
* **$47 million** in recovered annual revenue

---

## Why AI Outperformed Traditional Rules

Traditional fraud systems rely on static rules and manual intervention, making them slow to adapt to evolving fraud patterns.

Visa Decision Manager uses machine learning to proactively identify emerging threats, automate decisions, reduce customer friction, and improve operational efficiency.

---

## Conclusion

The SkyConnect Airlines case demonstrates how AI-driven fraud prevention can transform payment security. By adopting Visa Decision Manager, the airline reduced fraud losses, improved customer experience, streamlined operations, and recovered significant revenue—highlighting the strategic value of AI in modern payment ecosystems.
